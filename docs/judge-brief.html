🛡 Midnight Dual-Ledger Sealed-Bid & Policy-Lock Architecture

1. Pre-Award Policy Lock Issuer calls lock_compliance_policy() with private [min_band, max_band, policy_salt]; publishes only policy_commitment.
→
2. ZK Sealed Bidding Bidders call submit_sealed_bid() proving 0 < amount ≤ reserve_cap & unique identity; discloses persistentHash commit only.
→
3. Reveal & Award close_bidding() opens reveals; reveal_bid() verifies commitment preimage and deterministically selects lowest valid bid; finalize_award().
→
4. Compliance & Receipt prove_compliance_band() proves winning_bid ∈ [min, max] + locked commitment opening; settle_tender() + Offline Receipt v2.
